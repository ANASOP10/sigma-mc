Privacy Policy

Sigma.MC Discord Bot · Last updated: October 17, 2026

This policy explains what data Sigma.MC ("the Bot") collects, why, and how you can have it removed.

1. Data we collect

The Bot only stores the data it needs to work:

  • Discord IDs — user, server, channel, role and message IDs.
  • Server settings — e.g. welcome channel and message, auto-roles, ticket category and staff roles.
  • Tickets — who opened a ticket and when, and ticket messages if a transcript is saved by server staff.
  • Giveaways — the prize, end time and the IDs of users who entered.
  • Moderation records — warnings issued by server staff (user ID, reason, moderator ID and date).

We do not collect email addresses, passwords, IP addresses or payment information, and we do not read or store messages outside of the Bot's features.

2. How we use data

Data is used only to provide the Bot's features (for example, to give auto-roles, send welcome messages, run giveaways and manage tickets). We do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Storage and security

Data is stored on the Bot's hosting server and is accessible only to the developer. We take reasonable steps to protect it, but no system is 100% secure.

4. Data retention

Data is kept while the Bot is in your server. When the Bot is removed from a server, that server's data may be deleted. Giveaway and ticket data may be removed automatically after they end or close.

5. Your rights

You can ask us to view or delete the data the Bot holds about you or your server. Contact us using the details below and we will handle your request within a reasonable time.

6. Children

The Bot is not intended for anyone under Discord's minimum age. We do not knowingly collect data from such users.

7. Discord

The Bot runs on Discord, which has its own Privacy Policy. We are not responsible for how Discord handles your data.

8. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows when it last changed.
